Law & Order Awards Program
Palm Valley Post 233 began its support for this program in 2011 by requesting candidate nominations from both the St. Johns County Sheriff’s and Fire-Rescue Departments. We publicly honor the first responders of our community during the month of September in conjunction with Patriot’s Day. A complimentary dinner is served to all first responders who attend. To learn more about this program, click here to download a brochure. We will also accept your donation toward this program to help us defray costs involved with this endeavor of public recognition. Thank you.
